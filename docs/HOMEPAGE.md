# Homepage

The homepage is built for the plant floor: find the right instruction fast, get back to the ones you use every shift, and see what changed. Large text and touch targets work on tablets and shared workstations, in light and dark themes.

## Find an instruction

- The search box at the top searches part numbers, mold numbers and instruction titles as you type.
- **Scan a part label** (barcode scanners type into the focused box) or type a part number and press **Enter**. If exactly one instruction matches, it opens immediately; otherwise focus moves to the first result.
- Department chips under the search filter the results and every list on the page. Only departments with instructions are shown. Choose **All departments** to clear the filter.
- Matching controlled documents and formulas are listed under the instruction results.

Operators only see released instructions; drafts and pending revisions appear for authors and approvers.

## Quick actions

Shortcuts below the search depend on your role. Anyone who can submit instructions for review gets **New instruction**, **My drafts**, **Document center** and **Formula tools**. That is every role today, including operators (Contributors). An account without authoring rights would get **Document center**, **Formula tools** and **Process maps** instead.

## Lists

The default **Plant floor** layout shows:

| List | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| Pinned instructions | Instructions you pinned from the instruction page (**Pin** in its action menu). |
| Recently changed | The latest change to each instruction in the chosen time window. |
| New instructions | Instructions created in the last 7, 30 or 90 days. |
| Needs review | Instructions flagged by a user or past their review window. |

Each row shows the part number, revision, title, department and when it last changed; tap anywhere on the row to open it.

## Customize

Choose **Customize homepage** to add or remove lists, change widths, reorder them, or start from another layout (Balanced, Operations Live, Supervisor, Personal Focus, Compact Grid, Audit Review). The search box is always shown at the top and is not part of the layout.

Choose **Save layout** to keep changes; **Cancel** restores the saved layout. **Save as my default** stores a personal starting layout and **Load my default** previews it. **Reset layout** previews the standard Plant floor layout. Both preview actions can be undone and need **Save layout** to take effect. Layouts belong to your account and follow you across devices.

Widths (third, half, two-thirds, full) apply on large screens; lists use two columns on tablets where space permits and stack on phones. To rename a list, use its pencil button while customizing.

If another tab or device saved a different layout first, the editor keeps your draft and asks you to reopen it to load the newer layout. A connection timeout also keeps the draft available.
